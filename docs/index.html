Loqui

Press a shortcut, speak, and clean text appears wherever your cursor is.

Download for macOS
Free and open source · macOS 15+ · Apple Silicon & Intel

Your voice never leaves your Mac

Transcription runs entirely on-device using Apple's speech frameworks. There is no server, no account, and no telemetry — the only network request Loqui makes is checking this site for a new version.

What it does

  • One configurable global shortcut — tap to toggle, or hold for push-to-talk
  • Pastes straight at your cursor, in any app
  • On-device cleanup: filler words, capitalization, punctuation, custom dictionary
  • Local history with retention you control, and dictation stats
  • Signed, notarized, and self-updating

Built in the open

Loqui is GPL-3.0 licensed. Read the source, check the privacy claims against it, or build it yourself with one command — github.com/jameslaws/loqui.
