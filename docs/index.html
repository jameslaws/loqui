How it works

Three seconds to learn

Loqui lives in the menu bar. There is no window to open, no button to find, and nothing to set up beyond granting two permissions on first run.

1

Put your cursor somewhere

An email, a terminal, a chat box, a code editor. Anywhere you would normally start typing.

2

Press ⌘R and talk

That is the default and you can change it to anything. Tap once to start, or hold the key down for push to talk.

3

Your words appear

Tidied up and pasted at the cursor. No window to copy from, no step in between.

Dictate anywhere

One key, every app

Loqui types into whatever is in front of you, so it works in software that has never heard of it. It is a keystroke, not an integration.

  • ✓ Tap or hold. Tap to start and stop for long thoughts, or hold the key like a walkie talkie for quick ones.
  • ✓ Your shortcut, your choice. Any key with ⌘, ⌃ or ⌥, or a bare function key from F1 to F15.
  • ✓ It will not hijack your other shortcuts. Modifiers are matched exactly, so setting ⌘R leaves ⌘⇧R alone.
  • ✓ Never loses your words. Pastes at the cursor, and falls back to the clipboard if there is nowhere to paste.
  • ✓ Talk for as long as you need. No thirty second limit. Dictate a whole specification in one go if you want to.

Works in

Mail, Messages, Slack, Notes, Terminal, Xcode, VS Code, ChatGPT, Claude, Safari, Word, Figma, your CRM, that internal tool nobody maintains. If you can type in it, you can talk to it.

Clean output

Text you can send, not a transcript you have to fix

Raw dictation is full of the noise of speech. Loqui tidies it the instant you stop talking, using plain rules rather than a model, so it costs nothing and adds no delay.

  • ✓ Filler words removed. Um, uh, er, hmm and their variants. Only the unambiguous ones by default, so real words survive.
  • ✓ Sentences capitalised. Including the standalone "i" that every recogniser gets wrong.
  • ✓ Spacing and punctuation tidied. Double spaces collapsed, stray commas cleared, no space left before a full stop.
  • ✓ Optional spoken formatting. Say "new paragraph" or "new line" and get one. Off by default, because sometimes you mean the words.

Your vocabulary

It learns the words you actually use

Every recogniser mangles product names, client names and industry jargon. Add the mistake and the correction once, and Loqui fixes it every time from then on.

  • ✓ Anything Apple mishears, mapped to what you meant. Matched as whole words, in any case.
  • ✓ Starts empty. A fresh install carries nobody else's terms, and nothing leaves your machine.
  • ✓ Edits apply immediately. Add a term and the very next dictation uses it. No restart.

Dictation stats

See exactly what it is saving you

Loqui counts every dictation, so the benefit is a number you can check rather than a claim on a website. It counts words and durations, never what you said.

  • ✓ Time saved. Your speaking pace measured against typing, converted into hours you did not spend.
  • ✓ Today, this week, this month. Each compared with the period before it.
  • ✓ Charts by day, month, weekday and hour. Including the hours you dictate most.
  • ✓ Streaks, records and averages. Longest single dictation, highest volume day, words per active day.

History

Everything you said, for as long as you want it

Dictations are saved locally so you can find that thing you said an hour ago. How long they stick around is entirely your call, including not at all.

  • ✓ Search the lot. Full text search across everything you have dictated, with one click to copy it again.
  • ✓ Recent, straight from the menu bar. The last few dictations, one click from anywhere.
  • ✓ Delete anything, or all of it. Individually or in one go, and it is gone from disk, not just hidden.

Retention, in Settings

Choose how long Loqui keeps what you dictate. Set it to Off and it never writes your transcriptions to disk at all, while the stats keep working, because those only ever counted words.

Privacy

Your voice never leaves your Mac

Not a policy you have to trust. An architecture you can check, because the whole thing is open source.

  • ✓ Transcription runs on device. Apple's speech engine, locally. No audio is uploaded, because there is nowhere to upload it to.
  • ✓ Exactly one network request exists. Checking this site for a new version. That is the only connection in the codebase.
  • ✓ No analytics, telemetry or crash reporting. Not anonymised, not aggregated, not opt out. There is none.
  • ✓ No account, ever. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to log into, no email address collected.
  • ✓ Logs never contain what you said. Diagnostics record events and word counts only.
  • ✓ Signed and notarised by Apple. Updates are signed again on top of that, so a tampered build will not install.

Specifications

RequiresmacOS 15 or later. Best accuracy on macOS 26 and up, which uses Apple's newer speech engine; earlier versions fall back to the previous recogniser.
HardwareUniversal binary. Apple Silicon and Intel.
PermissionsAccessibility, to notice your shortcut anywhere and paste at the cursor. Microphone, to hear you. Nothing else.
Default shortcut⌘R, changeable to any key with ⌘, ⌃ or ⌥, or any function key.
Where it livesThe menu bar. No dock icon, no window unless you open one.
UpdatesAutomatic, via Sparkle. Signed and checked before anything installs.

PriceNothing. No trial, no tier, no upgrade prompt.

Prefer to build it yourself?

No Apple developer account required. You need Xcode, and one command does the rest.

git clone https://github.com/jameslaws/loqui.git
cd loqui
./install.sh

Stop typing what you could say

It takes about a minute to install and roughly three seconds to learn.
